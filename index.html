Our Story

A Café Built on
Compassion & Craft

Purrora began as a dream shared between two cat lovers and a barista — a space where specialty coffee and the calming presence of cats could coexist in genuine luxury. Every corner is designed for lingering.

All 24 of our resident cats are rescued and fully vetted. Many are available for adoption. Your visit doesn't just fuel your day — it changes theirs.
